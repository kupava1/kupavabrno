Kupava at the Lidový rok International Folklore Festival

On 5 September 2026 Kupava performed at the Lidový rok International Folklore Festival in Velká Bystřice.

Thank you for the invitation and the opportunity to be part of the Lidový rok International Folklore Festival in Velká Bystřice. We met ensembles from the Czech Republic, Slovakia, Mexico and Portugal and took home many wonderful memories.

We thank the whole organising team for their care and organisation.
