Christmas concert of national minorities at the Palace of Noblewomen

On 10 December 2024 Kupava performed at the Babylon Association’s Christmas concert in the chapel of the Palace of Noblewomen in Brno.

Polish and Ukrainian Christmas songs and carols filled the chapel of the Palace of Noblewomen. With this chamber concert the Babylon Association joined Brno’s events as the European Capital of Christmas 2024. Alongside Kupava, Jacek Slevčuk and Marie Kořenková performed.

We thank the Babylon Association for the opportunity to present Ukrainian culture in the Czech Republic.
