Concert for Life II live on TV Noe

On 6 April 2025 Kupava performed at St Wenceslas Church in Ostrava in the charity Concert for Life II, broadcast live by TV Noe.

It was a great honour to take part in a charity project supporting rehabilitation stays for war veterans and injured children at Darkov Spa. The evening was hosted by actress and presenter Sarah Haváčová and singer-songwriter Pavel Helan. We performed alongside the Czech-Ukrainian choir Sonjašnyk from Litomyšl and the Brno children’s choir Spivanočky.

Thanks to the audience’s donations, CZK 200,000 was raised for the rehabilitation of war veterans and injured children. Thank you to everyone who was with us, in person or at home.
