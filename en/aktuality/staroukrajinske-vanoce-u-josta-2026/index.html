Old Ukrainian Christmas at Jošt 2026

On Saturday 5 December 2026 Kupava will sing old Ukrainian carols and shchedrivky on Moravské náměstí in Brno.

The Ukrainian folk ensemble Kupava invites you to its Advent programme Old Ukrainian Christmas at Jošt. On Saturday 5 December 2026, traditional Ukrainian carols and shchedrivky will be sung in traditional polyphony, with live music and the Christmas star, by the statue of Jošt of Moravia on Moravské náměstí in Brno.

Old Ukrainian Christmas is a regular part of the ensemble’s repertoire – in recent years we have presented it at the Jošt statue, at St Joseph’s Church in Brno and in Vienna.
