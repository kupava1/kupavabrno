Kupava at the Smetana’s Litomyšl festival

On 27 June 2026 Kupava performed in the Festival Gardens programme of Smetana’s Litomyšl.

On Saturday 27 June 2026 we performed in the Festival Gardens programme of the Smetana’s Litomyšl festival, singing Ukrainian folk songs in traditional polyphony with live music.

We greatly value the opportunity to perform at one of the most important music festivals in the Czech Republic and thank the organisers for the invitation.
