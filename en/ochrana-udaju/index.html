Privacy policy

How Ukrajinský folklorní soubor KUPAVA, z.s. (the Kupava Ukrainian folk ensemble) handles the personal data of visitors to kupavabrno.cz and of people who contact us. Effective from 11 November 2026.

Data controller

Ukrajinský folklorní soubor KUPAVA, z.s., reg. no. 23686766, registered office Městečko 68, 664 61 Rajhrad, Czech Republic, registered with the Regional Court in Brno, section L, file 31074.

Contact: kupava@kupavabrno.cz, phone +420 777 072 433.

Contact form and e-mail

When you write to us via the form or by e-mail, we process your name, e-mail address, where given the name of your organisation or event, and the text of your message. We use these details only to reply and for follow-up communication, for example to arrange a performance. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering your enquiry, or steps prior to entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(f) and (b) GDPR).

We keep messages for as long as necessary to deal with them and no longer than three years after our last communication.

The form is delivered by the FormSubmit service (formsubmit.co), which forwards your message to our mailbox. The message passes through this service’s servers, which may be located outside the European Union.

Cookies, Google Analytics and Google Ads

We use Google Analytics 4 and the Google Ads tag provided by Google Ireland Limited to measure website traffic and the effectiveness of our ads (the Google Ad Grants programme for non-profits).

Analytics and advertising cookies are stored only with your consent, given in the cookie bar (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Without consent the website uses Google Consent Mode: no cookies are stored and Google receives only limited signals without identifiers, such as the fact that a page was viewed.

We do not use ad personalisation. Google Analytics data is kept for 14 months. Google may also process data in the USA; such transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time via the “Cookie settings” link in the footer. Your choice is remembered in your browser’s local storage.

YouTube videos

Video thumbnails are loaded from i.ytimg.com (Google). A video only starts after you click it, in YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). Only then does YouTube process data under its own policy.

Social networks

We only link to Facebook, Instagram and YouTube. We do not load their plugins or tracking code.

Website hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit, your IP address and technical browser data are processed as needed to deliver the page and protect against abuse (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Fonts are served directly from our own website.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict its processing and to data portability, the right to object and the right to withdraw consent at any time. Just write to kupava@kupavabrno.cz.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz).
